A template post: eigenvalues of a 2×2 matrix

Ketan Saichandran · 28 October 2026

This post exists as a template — copy this file to start a new one. It shows the page structure, inline math like $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 = \operatorname{tr}(A)$, a display equation, and the citation block every post ends with.

Setup

Let $A$ be a $2 \times 2$ real matrix,

$$ A = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}. $$

Its eigenvalues $\lambda$ satisfy $\det(A - \lambda I) = 0$, which expands to the characteristic polynomial

$$ \lambda^2 - (a+d)\lambda + (ad - bc) = 0. $$

Solving with the quadratic formula gives

$$ \lambda_{1,2} = \frac{(a+d) \pm \sqrt{(a+d)^2 - 4(ad-bc)}}{2}. $$

So the two eigenvalues sum to the trace, $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 = a + d$, and multiply to the determinant, $\lambda_1 \lambda_2 = ad - bc$.
