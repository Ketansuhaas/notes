Convexity and Jensen's inequality

Ketan Suhaas Saichandran
convexityoptimizationjensenprobability

Builds on: Taylor expansion, Expectation and variance algebra · Leads to: Entropy and cross-entropy, Primal and dual problems

Convex = bowl-shaped

Any chord lies above the graph; any tangent lies below it.

Chord$f(\lambda x+(1-\lambda)y)\le\lambda f(x)+(1-\lambda)f(y)$
Tangent$f(y)\ge f(x)+\nabla f(x)^T(y-x)$
Curvature$H(x)\succeq0$ everywhere (every direction curves up)

Examples

Convex: $x^2$, $e^x$, $-\log x$, $\|x\|$, $x_1^2+x_1x_2+x_2^2$ ($H$ has eigenvalues $3,1$). Not convex: $\sin x$, almost every neural-network loss.

Local is global

The tangent is a floor under the whole function. A flat tangent means nothing anywhere is lower.

$$\nabla f(x)=0 \ \Rightarrow\ f(y)\ge f(x)+0 \ \ \text{for every } y.$$

Jensen's inequality

For a bowl, the average of outputs beats the output of the average.

$$f \text{ convex}: \quad f(\mathbb{E}X)\le\mathbb{E}f(X). \qquad \log \text{ concave}: \quad \log\mathbb{E}X\ge\mathbb{E}\log X.$$

Proof: tangent at $\mu=\mathbb{E}X$ gives $f(X)\ge f(\mu)+f'(\mu)(X-\mu)$. Take $\mathbb{E}$; the last term averages to $0$.

Example

$f(x)=x^2$: $(\mathbb{E}X)^2\le\mathbb{E}X^2$, i.e. $\operatorname{Var}X\ge0$.

Derive it yourself

Prove $\mathrm{KL}(p\|q)=\sum_xp(x)\log\frac{p(x)}{q(x)}\ge0$.

Solution

$-\mathrm{KL}=\mathbb{E}_p\big[\log\tfrac{q}{p}\big]\le\log\mathbb{E}_p\big[\tfrac qp\big]=\log\sum_xq(x)=\log1=0$. Jensen, with $\log$ concave.
