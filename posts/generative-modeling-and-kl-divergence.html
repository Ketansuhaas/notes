Generative modeling: approximating pθ

Ketan Suhaas Saichandran
information-theorydeep-learninggenerative-modelingkl-divergencemaximum-likelihood

Builds on: Supervised learning as KL minimization, KL divergence, Convexity and Jensen · Leads to: The reparameterization trick

Generative modeling is supervised learning with the label removed: fit a distribution over $x$ itself. The objective is easy. Computing $p_\theta(x)$ is the hard part.

Same objective, one level up

Swap $p(y\mid x)$ for $p(x)$. Forward KL to the data is still maximum likelihood.

$$ \mathrm{KL}(p_{\text{data}}\|p_\theta)=\underbrace{-H(p_{\text{data}})}_{\text{const}}-\mathbb{E}_{x\sim p_{\text{data}}}[\log p_\theta(x)] \quad\Rightarrow\quad \min_\theta\mathrm{KL}\iff\max_\theta\,\mathbb{E}_{p_{\text{data}}}[\log p_\theta(x)]. $$

Example

Data: 7 heads in 10 flips, $p_\theta=\text{Bernoulli}(\theta)$. Maximize $7\log\theta+3\log(1-\theta)$: $\ \frac7\theta=\frac3{1-\theta}\Rightarrow\theta=0.7$.

Why it's hard

A density over images must integrate to $1$ over a huge space. That normalizer is an intractable integral.

$$p_\theta(x)=\frac{\tilde p_\theta(x)}{Z_\theta}, \qquad Z_\theta=\int\tilde p_\theta(x)\,dx \quad (\text{no closed form for a neural } \tilde p_\theta).$$

Every method below is a different way around $Z_\theta$.

One project in five disguises: fit $p_\theta$ to $p_{\text{data}}$.

Derive it yourself

VAE: $p_\theta(x)=\int p_\theta(x\mid z)\,p(z)\,dz$. For any $q(z\mid x)$, prove the ELBO:

$$\log p_\theta(x)\ \ge\ \mathbb{E}_{z\sim q}[\log p_\theta(x\mid z)]-\mathrm{KL}\big(q(z\mid x)\,\|\,p(z)\big).$$

Solution

Multiply and divide by $q$, then Jensen ($\log$ is concave):

$$\log p_\theta(x)=\log\mathbb{E}_{q}\Big[\frac{p_\theta(x\mid z)\,p(z)}{q(z\mid x)}\Big]\ \ge\ \mathbb{E}_q[\log p_\theta(x\mid z)]+\mathbb{E}_q\Big[\log\frac{p(z)}{q(z\mid x)}\Big].$$

The last term is $-\mathrm{KL}(q\|p(z))$. The gap is $\mathrm{KL}\big(q(z\mid x)\|p_\theta(z\mid x)\big)$: zero when $q$ is the true posterior.
